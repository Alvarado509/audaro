Privacy Policy

Short version: your audio never leaves your device. Long version: below.

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Your files

All Audaro tools run 100% in your browser using the Web Audio API. When you open an audio file in a tool, it is decoded and processed on your own device. It is never uploaded to a server, because Audaro has no server that receives files. When you close the page, the file is gone from memory.

What we collect

We do not run accounts, analytics that identify you, or tracking cookies. The site does not ask for your name, and the tools work without any personal information.

Premium waitlist

If you leave an email on the pricing page to be notified at the Premium launch, that email is stored only in your own browser (local storage) for now. It is not sent to any server, because there is no server endpoint yet. When Premium launches, we will explain exactly where that email goes before asking you to confirm anything.

Contact

If you email us, we receive the email you send — and only that. We do not share it, sell it, or add it to any list without your consent.

Advertising

The free plan is supported by ads. When ads are added, this page will be updated to name the ad provider and what it may collect. Today, there is no ad provider on the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: contact@audaro.site.
