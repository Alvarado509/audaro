• FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Audaro really free?

Yes. All seven audio tools are free to use, with no account, and files up to 100 MB on the free plan. Premium ($5/month) only adds comfort features: no ads, batch processing, and larger files.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs 100% in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your audio never leaves your device.

What audio formats can I use?

You can open MP3, WAV and OGG files in every tool. Results download as WAV or MP3.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Audaro works in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or computer — nothing to download, nothing to install.
