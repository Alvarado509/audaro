Talk to us.

A bug, a feature request, a tool you wish existed? Write to us — every message is read by a human.

Send us an email

The fastest way to reach us is email. Your email app will open with a ready-to-send message — nothing is sent automatically, and nothing you type here is stored on our servers (there are none).

Email contact@audaro.site {icon('mail', 18)}

This form does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app, because Audaro has no server to receive messages. Your text never leaves your device until you press send in your email app.

How fast do you reply?

We aim to answer within a few days. Audaro is a small project, and every reply comes from a real person.

Can I request a new tool?

Yes — that is the best kind of message. Tell us the tool, what you would use it for, and how often. Requests with a real use case go to the top of the list.
