    • FAQ

    Questions about audio joiner

    How do I merge MP3 files online for free?

    Drop the files in Audaro Audio Joiner, arrange them in order, click Join files into one, and download the merged result as MP3 or WAV.

    How many files can I join?

    There is no fixed limit. The only limit is your device's memory, because everything runs in your browser.

    Do the files need the same format?

    No. You can mix MP3, WAV and OGG files — they are all decoded to the same sample rate before joining.

    Is there a gap between the joined files?

    No. Files are joined end to end, with no added silence.
