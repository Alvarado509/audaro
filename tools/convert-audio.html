• FAQ

Questions about audio converter

How do I convert MP3 to WAV for free?

Open the MP3 in Audaro Audio Converter, choose WAV, click Convert file, and download the result. The file never leaves your device.

Which formats can I open?

MP3, WAV and OGG, plus other formats your browser can decode, such as M4A and FLAC in most browsers.

Can I export to OGG?

No. Exports are WAV (lossless) and MP3 (192 kbps) only — OGG export is not available in this version.

Does converting lose quality?

Converting to WAV is lossless. Converting to MP3 re-encodes at 192 kbps, which is transparent for most listeners.
