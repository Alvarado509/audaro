• FAQ

Questions about speed changer

How do I speed up an MP3 without changing the pitch?

Open the file in Audaro Speed Changer, set the speed to 1.5x or 2x, keep Preserve pitch on, click Change speed, and download the result.

What does preserve pitch do?

It changes the tempo without changing the pitch, using the SoundTouch algorithm in your browser. Voices stay natural instead of turning into chipmunks.

Can I slow audio down to transcribe it?

Yes. Set the speed to 0.5x or 0.75x with pitch preserved — words stay clear and easy to follow.

What happens if I turn preserve pitch off?

The speed change works like an old tape: faster sounds higher-pitched, slower sounds lower-pitched.
