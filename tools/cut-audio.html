• FAQ

Questions about cut audio

How do I cut an MP3 online for free?

Open the file in Audaro Cut Audio, drag on the waveform to select the part you want, click Cut selection, and download the result as MP3 or WAV.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no server limit, because nothing is uploaded. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory.

Will cutting change the sound quality?

No. Cutting keeps the original audio untouched; the MP3 export is encoded at 192 kbps and the WAV export is lossless.

Can I cut the middle out of a song?

Yes. Select the part you want to keep and cut it — or cut the song twice to keep two parts and join them with the Audio Joiner.
