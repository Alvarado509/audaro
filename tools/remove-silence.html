• FAQ

Questions about silence remover

How do I remove silence from a podcast?

Open the episode in Audaro Silence Remover, set the threshold to about -35 dB, click Remove silence, listen to the preview, and download the tightened file.

What threshold should I use?

Start with -35 dB. If quiet words disappear, lower it to -45 dB. If pauses remain, raise it toward -20 dB.

Will it cut words that start quietly?

It can, if the threshold is too high. That is why you always preview before downloading — and why a small 0.05 s padding is kept around each kept part.

Does it work on music too?

It works, but it is made for speech. On music, quiet intros and endings may be treated as silence.
