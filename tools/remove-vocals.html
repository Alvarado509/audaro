• FAQ

Questions about vocal remover

How do I remove vocals from a song for free?

Open the song in Audaro Vocal Remover and click Remove vocals. The tool uses phase cancellation to remove centered vocals, and you download the result as MP3 or WAV.

Does it work on every song?

No. It works best on stereo songs with centered vocals, and results vary. It is not studio-grade AI separation, and some vocals or instruments may remain.

Why does my file need to be stereo?

Phase cancellation compares the left and right channels. On a mono file there is nothing to compare, so the tool will tell you it needs a stereo file.

Can I use the result for karaoke?

Yes, that is the most common use. For best results, start with a good-quality stereo recording.
